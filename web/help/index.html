Get started

How to create an account
You can use Mise without an account. To create one, open the app, tap Create account on the sign-in screen, and enter your name, email and a password (8+ characters). In early access, accounts live on your device; sync across devices is coming.
How to add a recipe
Tap Add in the middle of the navigation bar, then:
  • Paste a recipe from anywhere — Mise splits it into steps and finds the timers.
  • Import with AI from a link, a photo, or messy text.
  • Or try the sample recipe to see how it looks.
What is the Mise Prompt?
A short instruction you paste into any AI chat after asking it for a recipe. The AI then replies in the exact format Mise reads perfectly. Find it in Account → Sous → Mise Prompt, or on the Add screen.
Where are my recipes saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes them, so keep a copy of favourites (for example by sharing them as a QR code). Sync across devices is coming.

Recipes

Make more or less
On a recipe, use − and + next to Makes, or tap the number to type an exact amount. Every ingredient and the amounts in the steps rescale.
Show a recipe in cups or grams
Pick As written, Metric or US above the ingredients (or in Settings → Units). It converts ingredients, steps and oven temperatures. Teaspoons and tablespoons stay the same, and small gram amounts stay in grams.
Missing an ingredient? Use a swap
Tap the ⇄ next to an ingredient to see common substitutes (buttermilk, eggs, butter, yeast and more). For anything else, ask Sous.
Edit a recipe or add a photo
Open a recipe and tap the pencil. You can change the photo, name, description, servings, ingredients, steps and notes. Timers are picked up from the step text (e.g. "bake 40 minutes").
Collections
Group recipes into collections like Weeknight or Baking: open a recipe and tap Add to collection. Collections appear as filters on the Recipes tab.

Cooking

Using cook mode
Tap Start cooking to see one big step at a time with its timers and ingredients. Swipe or tap Next step, tap a timer to start it, and the screen stays on. Prefer a list? Switch in Settings → Cook mode.
Mise en place (Prep)
Tap Prep to get everything ready first: preheat the oven, gather equipment, do the chopping, and put each ingredient on the counter. Tick things off, then start cooking.
Cook hands-free
Tap the mic in cook mode. Mise reads each step aloud and you move along by voice ("next", "back", "repeat", "start timer"). Turn on "Wait for Hey Mise" in Settings so chatter doesn't skip steps.
Your cooking journal & streaks
Finishing a recipe logs it. Rate it, add a photo and notes for next time — your notes show on step 1 the next time you cook it. A streak counts weeks in a row with at least one cook.
Plan when to start (Ready by…)
On a recipe, tap Ready by…, choose when you want to eat, and Mise works backwards to when each step should start. Tap Remind me for a nudge when it's time to begin.

Timers & alarms

Start, pause and add time
Start a timer from a cook-mode step, or on the Timers tab with the wheel. Run several at once; tap one to pause or resume, and use +1 min to add time. The next to ring shows at the top of every screen.
Will my timer ring if I leave the app?
On the phone app, yes — Mise sends a notification even when the phone is locked or Mise is closed. The web app rings while it's open. Allow notifications for Mise when asked.
I don't hear the alarm
Check notifications are on (Account → Notifications), turn your volume up and silent/Focus off, and pick a louder tone in Settings → Timers & alarms.

Planning & shopping

Plan your meals
Tap the calendar on the Recipes tab, pick a day, then add a recipe or a note like "Leftovers" to breakfast, lunch or dinner. Days with plans show dots. From a recipe, the calendar button adds it to any day.
How the shopping list works
Tap Add to shopping list on a recipe, or add a whole week from the meal plan. Matching ingredients combine and sort by aisle. Tap to tick off, swipe to remove, add your own, and share it.
What can I make?
On the Recipes tab, tap What can I make? and add what's in your fridge. Your recipes are sorted by how little you're missing. Salt, pepper, oil and water always count.
Scan packaged food
From the shopping list, tap the barcode button and point the camera at a package. You'll see its Nutri-Score, how processed it is, fat/sugar/salt levels, and allergens (from the free Open Food Facts database).

Sous (AI)

What can Sous do?
Sous is your cooking assistant. Ask about substitutions, timing, techniques or what to cook, by text or voice. Sous is in preview — chat and voice work, with real answers coming soon.
What Sous knows about you
Open Account → Sous settings → What Sous knows about you to see it word for word: your cooking level, diet and allergies, and (if you allow them) your recipes, meal plan and journal. Turn any of these off there.
Rename, pin or delete a chat
Open your chat history (swipe right in Sous, or tap the menu), then long-press a chat for Rename, Pin and Delete. In an open chat, tap ••• at the top.

Sharing

Share with a QR code
On the Account tab, tap the QR button and choose what to share: a recipe, a cookbook, your shopping list, a Sous chat or your kitchen setup. Everything is inside the code, so it works without internet.
Scan someone's code
On the QR screen, tap Scan someone's code and point the camera at their screen. You'll see what they shared and can save a copy. Mise only opens Mise codes.

Privacy & data

What Mise keeps and where
Your recipes, plans, journal, chats and settings are saved on your device. Your sign-in is kept in your phone's secure storage.
  • The nutrition scanner sends only the barcode to Open Food Facts.
  • Feedback you send includes the app version and phone type.
  • AI features send what you ask to Mise's server to get an answer.
See the Terms & Privacy Policy.

Account & plans

Switch accounts or sign out
On the Account tab, tap your name to add another account, edit your profile, or sign out. Signing out keeps your recipes, plans and journal on the device.
