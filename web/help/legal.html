The fine print

Terms & Privacy

A plain-English summary of how Mise works with your data. This is a starting template — have it reviewed before launch.

Your recipes stay with you

Mise keeps your recipes, meal plans, cooking journal, chats and settings on your own device. They are not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes this data, so keep a copy of anything you want to save.

What leaves your device

  • Nutrition scanner: when you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (a free, open food database) to look up the product.
  • AI features (Sous & Import with AI): the text you send is sent to Mise's server to generate a reply. Along with it we may include the preferences you set (such as diet) so answers fit you — you control this in Sous settings.
  • Feedback: when you send feedback or report an issue, your message, any screenshots you attach, the app version and your device type are shared with the Mise team.

Accounts

You can use Mise without an account. If you create one, we store your name, email and a securely hashed password so you can sign in. We never store your password in plain text. Sync across devices is coming later.

No tracking or ads

Mise does not sell your data and does not show third-party advertising.

Using Mise

  • Mise is provided "as is" while in active development. Features may change.
  • Cooking information, timings and AI answers are guidance — always use your own judgement, especially around food safety, allergies and the use of heat and sharp tools.
  • Don't use Mise to break the law or infringe others' rights.
