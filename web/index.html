Good to know

Common questions

Is Mise free?

Yes. The core app is free, and you can use it without an account. Paid plans with extra AI features are coming later.

Which phones does it work on?

Mise is built for iPhone and Android. Both are on the way — add your name on the store when it launches.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works on your device. Signing in (coming soon) will let your recipes follow you across devices.

Will timers ring if I leave the app?

Yes — timers send a notification even when the phone is locked or Mise is closed.

Is my data private?

Your recipes, plans and journal live on your device. See the Help center for details.
